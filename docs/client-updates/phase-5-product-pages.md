# Phase 5: Product Pages

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

The individual page a customer lands on for a single product:

- A **photo gallery** for the product.
- **Price, stock status, and key features** clearly laid out — including "Only 3 left" style low-stock warnings, and a disabled "Add to Cart" button with a "Notify Me" option for out-of-stock items instead of just hiding them.
- **Full description and compatible vehicle makes**, so customers can confirm a part fits their car before buying.
- **Customer reviews and ratings**, plus the ability for a customer to leave their own review.
- **Related products**, to encourage browsing beyond the one item.
- **Add to Cart and Save to Wishlist buttons.**

## Why it matters

This is the page that actually convinces someone to buy — it needs to answer "will this work for my car" and "can I trust this" clearly, with enough detail that customers don't need to message you to ask basic questions.
