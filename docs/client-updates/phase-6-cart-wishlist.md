# Phase 6: Cart & Wishlist Pages

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

The actual pages where the cart and wishlist "memory" built in Phase 2 becomes visible and usable:

- A **cart page** listing everything a customer has added, letting them change quantities or remove items, and showing a running subtotal, delivery fee, and total.
- A **cart drawer** — a quick-view panel that slides in from the side when something is added to the cart, so customers get instant confirmation without leaving the page they're on.
- A **wishlist page** for saved items.
- **Clear empty-state messaging** — if the cart or wishlist is empty, the page explains that plainly and points customers back to the shop, rather than showing a blank page.

## Why it matters

This is the last stop before checkout — customers need to easily double-check what they're buying, fix mistakes, and feel confident before moving forward.
