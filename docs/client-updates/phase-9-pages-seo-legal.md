# Phase 9: Info Pages, Search Visibility & Legal Pages

**Status: ✅ Complete**

## What this phase will build

- **About Us** — brand story and why LePlug exists.
- **Contact** — a contact form plus phone/WhatsApp/location details, with a map.
- **FAQ** — common questions about delivery, returns, payment, and warranty.
- **Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Returns & Refunds Policy** — real legal pages (using placeholder text until final legal wording is supplied).
- **A custom "page not found" screen** that matches the site's look, instead of a generic browser error.
- **Search engine and social sharing setup** — so the site shows up properly on Google, and looks right (with a title, description, and image) when a product link is shared on WhatsApp or social media.

## Why it matters

These pages don't sell products directly, but they're what makes a site feel legitimate and trustworthy — a working Contact page and real policies are often what a hesitant first-time customer checks before buying. The search/social setup also directly affects whether people can find the site in the first place.

## What you'll need to provide before this can be finalized

Real phone/WhatsApp number, business address and delivery area, operating hours, social media handles, and your registered business name for the legal pages. Placeholder text will be used until these arrive, so building can continue in the meantime.
