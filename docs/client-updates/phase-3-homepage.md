# Phase 3: Homepage

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

The homepage — the first thing most customers see:

- A **hero banner** with the brand statement and a single signature visual detail: a diagonal red stripe that "wipes in" when the page loads, echoing the red-earth rally roads the brand is inspired by. This is the one deliberate animated moment on the whole homepage — everything else stays calm and doesn't move until you interact with it.
- **Category tiles** — a visual way to jump straight into Exterior, Interior, Performance parts, and the rest.
- A **bestsellers section** highlighting popular products.
- A **"shop by vehicle make" strip** — logos for Toyota, Subaru, Nissan, and other brands you carry parts for, so customers who own one of those cars can find relevant parts quickly.
- **Trust signals** — short visual call-outs about delivery, warranty, and M-Pesa payment support.
- A **newsletter signup** section.

## Why it matters

The homepage sets the first impression — it needs to look premium and confident within a few seconds, without feeling like a generic template. It's also the main entry point into browsing, so how it's organized directly affects how easily customers find what they're looking for.

## A note on the vehicle-make logos

Using real manufacturer logos (Toyota, Subaru, etc.) carries some trademark risk since the site isn't an authorized dealer for those brands. This is being built with careful, informational wording ("Parts for your Toyota," not anything implying official partnership) and in a way that's easy to adjust per-brand if needed. It's flagged as a pre-launch legal checklist item, not something that blocks building the page now.
