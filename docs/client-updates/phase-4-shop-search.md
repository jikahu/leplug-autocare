# Phase 4: Shop, Categories & Search

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

The main product-browsing experience:

- A **full shop page** showing every product in a grid, with the category menu (built in Phase 2) now actually appearing above it.
- **Category pages** — e.g. a dedicated page for "Interior" showing only interior products, with a short intro description.
- **Filters** — narrow products down by category, subcategory, brand, price range, or vehicle make.
- **Sorting** — by price (low to high or high to low), newest, bestselling, or customer rating.
- **Search** — typing in the search bar (built in Phase 2) will actually return matching products by name, category, or brand.
- **Helpful "nothing found" messaging** — if a search or filter combination returns no results, the page will say so clearly and suggest what to try instead, rather than showing a blank confusing page.

## Why it matters

This is where most shopping actually happens. A customer who knows roughly what they want (a category, a brand, a budget) needs to get to the right products in a couple of clicks — not scroll through everything or hit a dead end.
