# Phase 8: Customer Accounts

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

- **Login and registration pages.**
- **Order history**, so customers can see what they've bought before.
- **Wishlist**, accessible from the account area.
- **Profile and saved addresses**, so returning customers don't have to retype their delivery details every time.

## Why it matters

Accounts turn one-time buyers into repeat customers — being able to reorder easily or check on a past order builds trust and convenience.

## Important: these are "practice" accounts, not a real login system yet

This phase builds the full experience of registering and logging in, but it's not connected to a real, secure authentication system yet — think of it as a realistic mockup that behaves correctly, rather than something ready to hold real customer passwords and data. Building real, secure accounts is a separate piece of backend infrastructure work that comes later, alongside the real payment processing from Phase 7.
