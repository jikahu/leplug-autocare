# Phase 10: Final Polish & Testing

**Status: ⏳ Planned**

## What this phase will build

A full pass over the entire site to make sure everything holds up under real-world use:

- **Checking every screen size**, from the smallest common phone screens up to large desktop monitors.
- **Keyboard accessibility** — making sure someone who can't use a mouse can still navigate the whole site.
- **Respecting "reduce motion" settings** for visitors who've asked their device to minimize animations.
- **Color contrast checks**, so text stays easy to read against the site's dark and light backgrounds.
- **Every "what if" scenario** — empty cart, empty wishlist, no search results, out-of-stock products, loading states, and broken links — all get a clear, helpful message instead of a blank or confusing screen.
- A final run through the full list of everything the site is supposed to do, confirming it's all actually working end to end.

## Why it matters

This is the difference between a site that *mostly* works and one that holds up no matter who's using it, on what device, or in what unexpected situation. It's the last step before the site is genuinely ready to show real customers.

## What's still separate from this phase

This phase doesn't include the pre-launch legal review of the vehicle-make logos (flagged in Phase 3), or connecting real payments and real accounts (flagged in Phases 7 and 8) — those are separate checklist items to close out before taking the site fully live to the public.
