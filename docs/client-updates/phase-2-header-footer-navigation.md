# Phase 2: Header, Footer & Navigation

**Status: ✅ Done**

## What this phase built

This phase built the parts of the site that appear on every single page, no matter which one a customer is looking at:

- **The header** — logo, main menu (Shop / About / Contact), a search bar, an account icon, and a cart icon that shows how many items are in the cart. The header stays pinned to the top of the screen as you scroll, and shrinks slightly once you start scrolling, so it stays out of the way without disappearing.
- **The mobile menu** — on a phone, the header simplifies down to just the logo, a search icon, the cart, and a menu ("hamburger") icon. Tapping the menu icon opens a full-screen menu, not a cramped little dropdown.
- **The footer** — organized into four sections (site info, shop categories, account links, contact & social), plus a bottom bar with copyright, legal page links, and payment method badges (M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard) shown as a trust signal.
- **Cart and wishlist memory** — when a customer adds something to their cart or saves it to their wishlist, it's remembered even if they close the browser and come back later. This is the invisible plumbing behind those features; the actual cart and wishlist pages come in Phase 6.
- **The category menu** for the shop section (a panel showing all 6 categories and their subcategories) — built now, but it won't actually appear on the site until the shop pages exist in Phase 4.

## Why it matters

The header, footer, and navigation are what make a site feel trustworthy and easy to use — customers expect to always know how to get to the cart, search for something, or get back home. Getting this right once, in one place, means it's consistent everywhere rather than needing to be rebuilt per page.

## What's still placeholder

- The **social media links** (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) in the footer currently go nowhere — they'll be connected to real accounts once you provide the handles.
- The **payment method badges** are simple text labels for now (M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard), not official logos — swapping in real logos is a quick follow-up once that's a priority.
- The **business name and copyright line** use "LePlug Autocare" as a placeholder pending your registered business name.
